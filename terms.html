Solia is a personal tracking and wellbeing tool. It is not a medical device and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

  • Cycle information is an estimate shown as a range, based only on what you log. It can be wrong, especially with irregular cycles.
  • Solia is not contraception and must never be used to prevent or plan a pregnancy. The optional fertile-window insight looks back at past cycles only and is not a prediction.
