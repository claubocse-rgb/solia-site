Made for feeling good with PCOS, not just tracking it.

Your irregular cycle is not an error. Solia gives you honest ranges instead of false predictions, a 30-second daily check-in, and meal and movement ideas matched to how you feel.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

🌙 Honest cycle ranges

Estimates built from your own history, shown as a range, never a fake "next period on the 12th". Irregular cycles are expected, not flagged.

📝 A 30-second check-in

One tap on a normal day. Otherwise: skin, energy, cravings, mood and more, on simple scales. Mood and energy are free forever.

📈 Patterns in plain words

"Days with less sleep had stronger cravings." Personal observations from your own journal, described honestly. They are not diagnoses and do not prove cause.

🌿 Meals and movement

Gentle ideas based on your energy, sleep, diet and allergies. A companion, not a drill sergeant, and never weight targets.

🩺 Ready for appointments

A factual PDF summary of your last 90 days and a list of your own questions to bring to your doctor (Premium).

🔒 Private by design

Optional app lock with Face ID, fingerprint or passcode, and discreet reminders that never show medication names.

Your journal lives on your phone. No account is needed. If you choose to sign in, you can keep a backup copy on EU servers (Supabase, Frankfurt) to move to a new phone. Photos never leave your phone. No ads, no analytics, no selling of data. Read the Privacy Policy.
