Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026 · Applies to the Solia app for iOS and Android and to this website.

The short version

  • Your journal lives on your phone. You can use Solia fully without an account. In that case we never receive your journal.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in with Apple or Google and back up, a copy of your journal (which includes health and wellbeing data) is stored on EU servers in Frankfurt (Supabase). Photos are never uploaded.
  • Purchases go through Apple or Google. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history so Premium works.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we never use it for advertising.
  • You are in control. Delete your backup, your account or all your data from inside the app at any time, or email us.

1. Who we are

Solia is published by Clau Bocse, a private individual based in Romania ("we", "us"). For personal data processed through the optional account and cloud backup, we are the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact for anything about privacy: contact@kromotec.com.

2. How Solia works

Solia is local-first. Everything you log is saved in a database on your phone. There is no sign-up when you install the app, and every feature works without an account. Data only leaves your phone in three situations, all described below:

  1. you choose to sign in and back up your journal (section 4);
  2. you buy or restore a subscription (section 5);
  3. you choose to share something yourself, for example a PDF report sent through your phone's share sheet.

3. Data that stays on your phone

When you use Solia without an account, the following is stored only on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it:

  • cycle and period entries, and daily symptom check-ins (for example skin, energy, cravings, pain);
  • mood and energy check-ins, sleep times, movement minutes;
  • medications you add (name, optional dose, reminder time) and when you mark them as taken;
  • appointments and other events, questions you prepare for a consultation, and journal notes;
  • your profile: optional first name, birth year, height and weight (weight is optional and never used for targets), goals, diet, allergies, activity and fitness level;
  • photos in the photo journal, with their date, area (skin or hair) and optional note;
  • settings such as theme, app lock, discreet notifications and the optional fertile-window insight.

The doctor report (PDF) is created on your phone. It only goes somewhere if you share it yourself.

4. Optional account and cloud backup

In Settings › Account & cloud backup you can sign in with Apple (iPhone) or Google. The only purpose of the account is to keep a backup copy of your journal so you can restore it on a new phone.

What we receive when you sign in

  • your email address (with Apple, this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email");
  • an account identifier from Apple or Google, and an internal user ID;
  • basic profile details your provider includes in the sign-in response (Google may include your name and profile picture). We do not use these for anything;
  • technical sign-in records kept by our hosting provider for security, such as sign-in times and IP address.

What the backup contains

When you tap Back up now, a snapshot of your journal is uploaded. This includes health and wellbeing data. Specifically:

  • your journal: symptom check-ins, notes, cycle and period entries;
  • mood and energy check-ins, sleep and movement entries;
  • medications, doses, reminder times and intake history;
  • appointments, events and consultation questions;
  • your profile, including the optional name, birth year, height and weight, goals, diet and allergies, and your app settings;
  • photo-journal notes (date, area, file name and your note), but not the photos themselves. Photo files never leave your phone.

The backup is stored by Supabase in its Frankfurt, Germany (EU) region. Each new backup replaces the previous one; we keep one backup per account. We do not look at backup contents, except where you ask us to help with your data (for example a deletion request) or where the law requires it.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Solia Premium is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle payment and billing as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card details or billing address.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous app user ID created on your phone (it is not linked to your Solia account or your email);
  • your purchase history for Solia from Apple or Google (products, dates, trial status, renewals, receipts);
  • basic technical data needed to answer the request, such as platform, app version, country or currency, and IP address.

RevenueCat does not receive any journal or health data.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics and no crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • No advertising and no advertising or attribution SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no advertising identifier is collected.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data, health data included, with anyone, for any purpose.
  • No profiling or automated decisions that affect you legally or in a similarly significant way. The patterns Solia shows are calculated on your phone, for you only.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent.

7. Notifications, app lock, photos and the widget

  • Notifications are local. Reminders (medication, morning check-in, wind-down, weekly recap, appointment prep) are scheduled on your phone. We do not use a push-notification server and do not collect push tokens. With Discreet notifications on, reminders never show medication names.
  • App lock uses your phone's own Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Solia never receives biometric data, only a yes/no result from the system.
  • Camera and photo library are used only when you add a photo to the photo journal. Copies are saved in Solia's private folder on your phone.
  • The Android widget lets you check in from the home screen. It reads and writes the same local database.

8. Your phone's own backups

Separately from Solia's optional cloud backup, your phone's system backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Google's Android backup) may include Solia's local data, depending on your phone settings. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by us. You can manage them in your phone's settings.

11. How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete it (Settings › Delete all my data) or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backup: until you replace it with a new backup, delete it, or delete your account.
  • Account: until you delete it in the app or ask us by email.
  • Hosting provider records and backups: deleted data can remain for a short time in Supabase's security logs and routine backups before it is overwritten under the provider's schedule.
  • Subscription records: RevenueCat keeps purchase records under its own retention rules; Apple and Google keep transaction records as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than 3 years unless the law requires more.

12. Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is protected with TLS (HTTPS) encryption in transit. Our hosting provider, Supabase, encrypts stored data at rest with AES-256 by default. Database rules (row-level security) let each account read and change only its own backup.

To be clear about the limits: the backup is not end-to-end encrypted. Someone with administrative access to our Supabase project could technically read it. Only the publisher has that access, and we do not look at backups except as described in section 4. On your phone, you can add a layer of protection with App lock. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

13. International transfers

Your cloud backup is stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Supabase is a company based outside the EU; any access by Supabase or its sub-processors from outside the European Economic Area is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) included in its data processing agreement.

RevenueCat is based in the United States and processes data in the US. These transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses included in RevenueCat's data processing addendum. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

14. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Access and export your data. On your phone, your data is always visible in the app, and Premium users can export a PDF summary. For a copy of what we hold in the cloud (your account and backup), email us.
  • Correct your data by editing it in the app, then backing up again, or by asking us.
  • Delete your data:
    • Settings › Delete all my data erases everything on your phone (journal, photos, reminders, profile). If you are signed in, it asks whether to delete your cloud backup too.
    • Settings › Account & cloud backup › Delete my account permanently deletes your account and your cloud backup.
    • No longer have the app? See Delete your account to request deletion by email.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (see section 10).
  • Restrict or object to processing, and data portability (we provide your backup as a JSON file).

To use any right by email, write to contact@kromotec.com, ideally from the email address of your Solia account so we can confirm it is you. We reply within one month (this can be extended by two months for complex requests, and we will tell you if so). It is free.

Because RevenueCat only holds an anonymous ID, we cannot find your subscription record from your email alone. If you want it deleted, include the order or transaction ID from your Apple or Google receipt.

15. US state privacy laws

We aim to meet US state consumer-health-data laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in Nevada and Connecticut, on a best-effort basis, and we give everyone the same rights. In particular:

  • we collect consumer health data only to provide the features you use, and only with your consent where required;
  • we do not sell consumer health data and do not share it with third parties other than the processors listed above;
  • we do not use geofencing or location data;
  • you can confirm, access, delete and withdraw consent as described in section 14. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; we will respond to an appeal within 45 days.

Read our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

16. Children

Solia is not intended for anyone under 16. In US states where the law requires it for processing health data, you must be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
