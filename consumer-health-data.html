Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026 · For residents of Washington, Nevada and other US states with consumer health data laws.

The short version

  • Your health data stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up.
  • We never sell it and never share it with third parties other than our backup host.
  • You can access it, delete it and withdraw consent at any time.

Categories of consumer health data we collect, and why

We only receive consumer health data if you create an optional account and back up your journal. The backup can include: symptom and cycle entries, mood and energy, sleep and movement, medications and intake history, appointments and consultation questions, journal notes, photo-journal notes (not photos), and profile details such as birth year, height and optional weight, goals, diet and allergies.

Purpose: to store a backup copy so you can restore your journal on a new phone. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Sources

Directly from you, through what you enter in the app and choose to back up.

Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with third parties or affiliates. The backup is stored by our processor Supabase (hosting and database, Frankfurt, EU), which processes it only on our instructions. We have no affiliates.

How to exercise your rights

  • Confirm and access: email contact@kromotec.com for a copy of your backup and a list of who processes it.
  • Delete: in the app, Settings › Account & cloud backup › Delete my account, or see Delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your cloud backup in Settings › Account & cloud backup, or email us.
  • Appeal: if we decline your request, reply to our answer to appeal. We respond within 45 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state Attorney General.

We respond to requests within 45 days. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
