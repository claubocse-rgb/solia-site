Delete your Solia account and data

Solia: PCOS Wellbeing & Cycle, published by Clau Bocse · Updated 7 October 2026

The short version

  • In the app: Settings › Account & cloud backup › Delete my account deletes your account and cloud backup immediately.
  • On your phone: Settings › Delete all my data erases everything stored on the phone.
  • No app any more? Email contact@kromotec.com and we delete your account within 30 days.
  • Subscriptions are separate: cancel them in your Apple or Google account.
Most people never create an account. If you never signed in, all your data is only on your phone and deleting it there (option B below) or uninstalling the app removes it. We have nothing to delete.

Before you start: cancel your subscription

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel Premium. Cancel it first in your Apple ID subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions (or in the app: Settings › Subscription › Manage subscription).

A. Delete your account and cloud backup (in the app)

  1. Open Solia and tap Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Account & cloud backup. Make sure you are signed in.
  3. Scroll to Leaving Solia? and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with Delete account. You will see "Your account and cloud backup have been deleted." and you are signed out.

This permanently deletes your Solia account (email and sign-in identities) and your cloud backup from our servers. The data on your phone stays until you delete it with option B.

Only want to remove the backup but keep the account? Tap Delete cloud backup on the same screen.

B. Delete all data on your phone (in the app)

  1. Open Solia and tap Settings.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm with Delete everything.
  4. If you are signed in, Solia asks what to do with your cloud backup: Delete backup too or Keep backup. Either way you are signed out on this phone.

This erases your journal, medications, events, photos, reminders and profile from the phone, and Solia starts again from setup. It does not close your account. To close it as well, use option A first.

C. Request deletion by email (if you no longer have the app)

  1. Email contact@kromotec.com with the subject "Delete my Solia account".
  2. Send it from the email address you used to sign in (with Sign in with Apple and "Hide My Email", tell us the relay address shown in your Apple ID settings). If we cannot match the address, we may ask a question to confirm it is you.
  3. We delete your account and cloud backup and confirm by email within 30 days (usually much sooner).

If you also want your subscription record removed from RevenueCat, include the order or transaction ID from your Apple or Google receipt, because that record is anonymous and not linked to your email.
